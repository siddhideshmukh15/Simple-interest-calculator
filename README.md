Simple Interest Calculator

A simple Simple Interest Calculator built using Python.
This beginner-friendly project calculates simple interest and the total amount based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period entered by the user.

Features

- Accepts the principal amount
- Accepts the annual interest rate
- Accepts the time period in years
- Calculates simple interest
- Calculates the total amount
- Displays the results clearly

Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest
